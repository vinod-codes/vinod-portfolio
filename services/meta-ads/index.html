How it works

Meta Ads management that starts with the result you want

Meta delivers your ads to the people most likely to do the thing you optimise for. Optimise for likes and you get likes. So the first job is choosing the right goal, a lead, a message or a booking, and making sure Meta can see it happen.

That means the Pixel on your site, the Conversions API sending the same events from the server so fewer are lost, and lead forms or WhatsApp chats set up to ask one qualifying question. Then we test a handful of ads against one goal and move budget to the winners.

Every month you see spend, leads, customers and cost per customer, not reach and impressions.

Questions

Meta Ads questions.

Should I boost posts?

Rarely. Boosting is built around a post. For enquiries, a campaign in Ads Manager optimised for leads or messages usually works better, because Meta optimises delivery for the result you choose.

How long before I know if it works?

Meta’s guidance is to wait for roughly 50 optimisation events after the last significant edit before judging costs. With a small budget that can take a couple of weeks, so we plan tests around it.

Lead forms or WhatsApp?

Forms suit offers where you need details up front. WhatsApp suits offers that close in a conversation. I’ve written up when each one wins in the notes.
