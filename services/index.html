04 · Tracking

Every lead gets a source.

Without tracking, ad platforms can’t learn and you can’t decide where the next rupee goes. Tracking setup covers the Meta Pixel and Conversions API (deduplicated, so each lead counts once), Google Ads conversions, GA4 with sensible events, and a UTM naming system for every link you post.

The end result is one monthly report that answers a single question: what did each customer cost, and which ad, post or search brought them?

How I work

Four steps. No mystery.

Each step is a fixed part of every project, in this order.

  1. Step 01

    Listen

    15-minute call. Your goal, your numbers, what you’ve tried.

  2. Step 02

    Fix the base

    Tracking, landing page, offer. Before any spend.

  3. Step 03

    Test

    3–6 ads, one goal, 1–2 weeks.

  4. Step 04

    Scale what works

    Move budget to the winners. Report cost per customer monthly.
